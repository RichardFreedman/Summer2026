Acknowledgments.

The thanks of the Hospital Committee for kind assistance rendered at this Concert are due to

Mesdames Elise Wiedermann, Marguerite Henderson, Meta Blaubaum and Herr Eduard Scharf.

The Members of the Melbourne Liedertafel and Associated Ladies' Choir.

The Members of the Orchestral Class, Conservatorium of Music, Melbourne University.

To the Melbourne Philharmonic Society
for the loan of the score, orchestral and choral parts of the "Schicksalslied," and kettledrums.

To the Royal Metropolitan Liedertafel
for the loan of score and orchestral parts of "Lohengrin."
