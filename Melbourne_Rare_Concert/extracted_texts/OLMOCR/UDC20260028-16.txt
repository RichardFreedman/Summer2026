Tonight! The Incomparable
ONLY AN ORPHAN GIRL
Being an oriful and elegant reconstruction of the celebrated drama by Henning Nelms
Presented By
The Madang Dramatic Society
At The
HOTEL MADANG
With a distinguished cast of well loved stellar performers from all over, including:
* Mrs. Belle Meek    Mrs. Lorraine George    Mr. Gavin Miller    Mr. Noel Henderson
Mrs. Shirley Richardson    Mrs. Caroline Crisp    Miss Rosemary Sutton *
Mr. Ron Gibbs has been employed to devise the action of the drama, which will be executed by the full strength of the company and the:
BARBER SHOP QUARTETTE.
Madang's choice illustrious vocalisers. Incidental solos by
Mr. Vin Smith,    Mr. Laurie Morgan    Mr. Ken Collier,    Mr. Trevor Parr
The scintillating and tuneful musical investiture has been composed and arranged in its entirety by Mrs. Elizabeth Brumby and will be conducted by her in person at each and every performance in all its symphonic splendour.
The realistic stage presentation here tonight is due to the indefatigable efforts of Mr. Lloyd George
INTERMISSION
Patrons are asked to note that light refreshments will be available at the saloon during intervals
The management has gone to great pains to insure that the DRAMA is presented with ALL the effects that contributed so largely to its tremendous BROADWAY SUCCESS and have therefore engaged Mrs. Tessa Gibbs for props. and prompts.
The whole effect of the wonders of illumination has been achieved by the efforts of Mr. Len Burley with the sole use of ELECTRIC TY.

We are proud to be associated with the Madang Amateur Theatrical Society in bringing to you, for the first time in Madang, a taste of Theatre Restaurant entertainment.

We are aware of the need to provide entertainment for the citizens of Madang and are deeply appreciative of the time that has been given by the producer, the players and the back stage personnel who have presented "Only An Orphan Girl" tonight.

It is our wish to take every opportunity to promote "live" entertainment, particularly if we can assist a local body at the same time.

We trust that you have thoroughly enjoyed "Only An Orphan Girl" and hope that we may once again invite you to join us "Theatre Restaurant Style".

Hotel Madang
