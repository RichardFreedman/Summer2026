Some Recent Press Notices

Berliner Tageblatt
"Mr. A. M. Henderson gave a masterly Recital of a very fresh and attractive programme of 'Old English Keyboard Music' (from Byrd to Arne)."

Paris, Le Monde Musical
"Mr. A. M. Henderson proved himself a pianist of musicianship and refined sensibility."

London Musical Opinion
"Perhaps the most delightful part of Mr. Henderson's lectures was when he played examples of each great master's style: his superb artistry and brilliant virtuosity made us wish that he gave us more opportunities of hearing him play in London."

London Musical Standard
"Mr. A. M. Henderson's Lecture-Recital on 'Modern Russian Composers' proved educative and enjoyable in the highest degree."

London Musical Times
"Mr. A. M. Henderson of Glasgow University gave a delightful Lecture-Recital on Russian Composers for the Pianoforte."

Newcastle Chronicle
"The Lecture-Recitals of Mr. A. M. Henderson of Glasgow University, the distinguished musical scholar and cultured pianist, are always very delightful, and last night's discourse at the Literary and Philosophical Society was no exception. The lecturer kept his audience genuinely interested and charmed."

Glasgow News
"Mr. A. M. Henderson is a pianist of shining ability who invariably charms and delights his audience by his interpretations of the Great Masters. His performance of Bach, Brahms and Chopin last night revealed his fine qualities of touch and execution, and the poetic animation of his readings."
