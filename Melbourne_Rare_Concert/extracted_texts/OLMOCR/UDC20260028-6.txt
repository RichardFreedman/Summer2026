La la la la la la!
We come in melodious measure,
O lady fair thy praise to sing:
'Twere us to a gladness to bring
Unto thy soul one thrill of pleasure.

What tho' with guarded door thou meet us,
If only thy heart open be?
'Tis all we are asking of thee
That thy rosy lips smile to greet us.

Bacchus: Ye lords, whose vinous treasure
Should mankind's woes allay,
I've called you in displeasure
Before my hook to-day.
The world more melancholy
And tedious daily grows,
And more the people's folly
That from the winecup flows.
The people drudge and labour,
Debited as they go,
The sword rules and sabre,—
'Tis pity to behold!
Such misery and detraction,
By Syr T. III. no more!
Back to our gay subjection
The world we must restore.
And therefore, children of the berry,
Ye wines both red and white, all take heed—
A King we'll choose both wise and merry,
So haste, approach, and to the poll proceed!

Chorus of Wines: Great Bacchus, hail!
Thy plan is wise, it cannot fail.
Our faith we plight by red and white,
The best of wines, wherever grown,
We loyally as king shall own.

Bacchus: See Don Sherry then, Spain's redoubtable knight,
He first appears your kind votes to invite;
By churchmen and laymen he e'er was beloved,
By the fair and the Princes Allured,
And e'en Isabella, the fond-hearted queen,
Regarded our knight with affectionate mien.
Where to sound of castanet
Gather the choicest of the grape,
Where the sun doth never set,
Choose ye there the King of Wines?

The cold blast of winter why heed we,
If only thy bright face be near,
And with thy music, what cheer!
Here standing at thy door what need we?
Of the brightness that spring-time bringeth
Thine abode at all times can tell,
And fair lady, where thou dost dwell
Ever the nightingale singeth.
And to know is thy bosom longing
What gleams in all these may be.

"To the Sons of Art"

Soli, Messrs. T. Jack, S. Fripp, S. J. Bruce and A. E. Johns.

Liedertafel

O Sons of Art, man's dignity to you is given:
Preserve it man!
It falls with you, with you ascends to heaven!
The hallowed themes of Magian dreams,
Founded in woman's vast creation,
Gliding like rivers find their ocean,
That great harmonious plan.

Eternal truth tho' oft rejected,
Exists not ever unprotected;
She finds a refuge with a truthful throng,
She turns not from all her glory,
Mighty when veil'd in mystic story;
She wakes the lay of lofty voices,
And over all her foes rejoices,
Her vengeance flashing, reeks in song.

"The Beleaguered"

Liedertafel

Fling wide the gates! come out!
Dauntless and true;
Brothers, of heart be stout,
We must not go
Bring from the battlements
Our flag again;
Tho' by the leaguer rent
It hath no stain.

Mothers and wives to pray'r,
From this eve,
The Lord of Hosts will care
For all we leave.
Plead that we sought not fight,
Nor chose the field;
But ev'ry free heart's right
We dare not yield.

"Bacchanalian Election"

Solo by Mr. Gladstone Wright.

Liedertafel

Chorus: Oh no, no, no!
A Philistine and bigot he,
Our king Don Sherry cannot be!

Bacchus: Preferreth then Tokay, the valiant Magyar,
Who has off prov'd his mettle in love and in war;
The spirit of fortune audacious and bold,
Carried by the hand of the young and the old.
Marianas—a many his honey have sipp'd,
And sturdy young Wenceles he many has tripp'd.
He drinks, he drinks, he drinks, he drinks,
Fiddling his fantastic lays,
Spurs the nut-brown maidens wear—
Will ye choose your monarch there?

Chorus: Oh no, no, no!
The Baron of Grünberg by far is he—
Tokay our king can never be!

Bacchus: The Baron of Grünberg now comes to view.
Whoever molesteth him, his boldness shall rue;
A 'cion of old and audacious name,
His "vinegar aspect" belies not his fame.
No enemy yet could against him prevail—
He drank, made grimaces, and so quickly turned tail.
That if a monarch you select
This candidate sardonically
Your stoutest foes you may defy,
They'll conquer, too, and conquer'd fly!
Ye, too, O King, a king elect,
Will prove a bitter toad.

Chorus: No, no, no, no!
This is a false deceitful wine,
He never shall be king of mine!

Bacchus: Behold then the Freuchman! Pretender in chivalry!
The lord of Champagne is, in all men's belief,
The elements four as his sponsors have stood,
And lavished their gifts in a generous mood.

"GOD SAVE THE QUEEN"

That come to say good-night to thee
And beneath thy window are thronging.
List then to what our song discovers:
With a blush she doth closely hide,
Thus join'd lovers we desire,
We are rovers, yes, gallant rovers,
For our gentle courtesy noted,
Two deities ruling our hearts,
To woman unto us devoted.
Ah! la la la la la! Ah! la la la la la!

Stephen Adams

To your free mother homage render,—
Boldly to gain her height aspire;
Enthroned she dwells in radiant splendour
No other throne is like her desert.
While you her thousand paths are tracing
Press onward, keeping Truth in sight;
Come altogether, stand embracing
Before the Throne where paths unite!

Mothers and wives, to prayer
Relief is nigh,
For you each man will dare
Deeds not die.
For sure as fire doth blaze,
Or foaming wine,
You shall to-night upraise
Songs of the free.

Kässmayer

From Fire he had the fervid glow
That animates his flow;
And Water gave the beads that swim
Like pearls on goblet's rim.
The Air gave him the golden dress,
The garb of joyousness;
And Air gave him the spirit free,
That makes him fly.
Piff, paff! see the froth
Mounting in the cup, sir!
Piff, paff! be not loth,
Quickly fill again, sir!
Fill again, drink again,
Sparkling vintage of Champagne—
Crown him king and let him reign
O'er all the world, sir!

Chorus: No! 'tis an uproarious wine,
A bragart and vain-glorious wine!
Howe'er he fume and rave!
He fills your glass to half, the knave!
A knave that will conquer's bent,
But whose elixir is quickly spent.
No, no! Champagne
As King of Wines shall never reign!

Bacchus: Then lo! by the Rhine-river's beautiful
Behold the vine of Rauenthal grow!
From pure Teutonic blood 'tis sprung,
Make glad the heart, the spirit young—
The noble Rhine's vines—
Shall be as King have sway!

Chorus: In crystal green the golden wine,
The heart's blood of dear Father Rhine,
Yes, sent the strain of land and sea
This shall be King! O! our joy be!
All joyous hearts in concord sing,
The glorious Rhine-wine shall be King!
