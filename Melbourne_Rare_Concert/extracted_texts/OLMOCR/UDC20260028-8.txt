Part Song

"For Home and Liberty"

LIEDERTAFEL

Hark, brothers, hark! the trumpet's warning blast!
Arm, brothers, arm! the foe is gather'ring fast;
The dawn of the day's at hand,
Fierce muttering comes,
With tramp of hurrying feet;
And the senses reel
In the tumult's wild,
Where the flashing squadrons meet.

Fight, brothers, fight for home and liberty!
Charge, brothers, charge; the hirelings turn and flee.
Like avalanches they rush,
From the ice-king's world,
With thunderous crash we go;
And the mighty host
Of the Tyrant's boast
Has melted away like snow.

Mourn, brothers, mourn for those who bravely fell:
Pray, brothers, pray for those who fought so well.
In their graves we laid
As they nobly died
With face to the foe, they're laid;
We will carve each name
In the stone of fame.
Their memory shall not fade.

March, brothers, march in sight of home again;
Sing, brothers, sing a loud triumphant strain;
Let our lov'd ones hear
The notes of the new song;
For which they have yearn'd so long;
At each hill and dale,
And each hill and dale,
Resound with the joyful song!

Cantata

"Festgesang"

LIEDERTAFEL

Let all creation praise the Lord,
Ye nations, bow before Him;
In choral strains with one accord,
Exalt Him and adore Him.
Ye orbs of day that roll in light,
Ye beams of morn from morn to night,
O praise your great Creator!
Angel hosts repeat the song,
Ye prophets clad in glory,
Ye nations, raise your voice prolong.
Repeat the wondrous story;
Ye spirits, who are flames of fire,
Awake and sing, and stirke your lyres,
O praise your great Creator!

Let our theme of praise ascending,
Blest in music's lofty strain,
Soaring through the starry main,
Peal in echoes never ending.
Lovingly did, its light arose;
Thus the sun and moon arise,
Faith and Hope began to banish
Doubt and soul-appealing fear.
Spirits of light, your beams so clear,
Error in their beams will vanish
Mortals roam'd without a guide,
Dread the night and shun the region;
Not a ray could be descried,
All was gloom and desolation,
Till the earth with one accord
Shall adore and praise the Lord.

The Word went forth, the Word was Light;
God spoke, His radiance darted.
Light made chaotic darkness bright,
And night when the light was parted.
In space the starry hosts are sung,
The Universe in music sung
Its great Creator's glory
Still in their courses orbs above,
Hail Him in concert as they move,
His praise in everlasting song
Rings through the vast celestial throng.
Soon may we join the lay,
In His presence, in His praises,
And stand in bright array
To harp and sing our praises.
Where shining Cherubim,
And seraphim blend,
Their hallelujahs blend
In strains that never end.

Part Song

"Alpine Night"

LIEDERTAFEL

Night around is gently closing,
As the light of day doth fail
Every sorrow's dispelling:
See the fire in the Alpine vale.
See, the meadows darkly shaded
Bid thee, Wanderer, depart;
Alpine light with thee is hid,
Ah, how beautiful thou art.
Softest winds are idly blowing,
Like elves' music in the trees.
Thou, the silver-clad mountains lowing
Hoodoos amongst the breeze.
Swift the eagle homeward flying,
Stirs the slumbering ev'ning air,
Alpine night, in shadow lying,
Thou art fair, ay, passing fair!

Like grim giants darkly frowning,
Mountains rise, steep and clear.
But the stars in their heights are crowning
And the stars are bright and clear.
And a fitful light is gleaming
On the lake's silvered outset,
Alpine night so full of dreaming,
Thou art passing and fair!

When the pale moon mutely gazes
On the scene so still, so fair,
In the east a bright sight she raises,
Like a pious and reverend pray'r.
From my eyes the tears are flowing,
Rest, oh! rest thou too, my heart!
Alpine night, with beauty glowing,
Ah, how fair thou art!

Part Song

"Sunrise"

LIEDERTAFEL

The starlight pale is waning,
Ere long will the dawn appear,
The deepest silence is ringing,
From the woods no sound I hear.

What joys and what griefs will be given,
Ere this coming day is done.
Let me raise then my heart to Heaven,
In pray'r from the mountain lone.

Alone on the hilltop I wander,
And watch thro' the chill night air,
In the dark horizon yonder,
The light that will glimmer there.

The sigh of the forest grows stronger;
Morning light suddenly breaks.
The lark's song grows louder and longer,
From night's slumber Earth now awakes.

While here in darkness lonely,
I wait for the coming ray,
One voice I hear, and one only,
'Tis the torrent far away.
