Dear Sir or Madam,

As announced at the last Concert of the Melbourne Liedertafel, it is the intention of the Society to tender the Conductor, Mr. August Siede, a Complimentary Concert on the eve of his departure for Europe, and I trust that you will accord this movement your patronage, and the loyal support which it merits.

This will be the first time in the musical annals of Australasia that an Australian Composer and Conductor has been sent to the artistic centres of the Old World to develop those natural gifts which it is acknowledged Mr. Siede possesses: and I am pleased to state that our distinguished visitor, Madame Melba, has shown her interest in this project by assuring us of her heartiest support, and has also signified her intention of being present at the Concert.

The programme, which will be compiled on popular lines, will be contributed by a number of our leading artists, and the full Choir and Orchestra of the Liedertafel; and considering the short notice that is necessarily given to the public, I beg to emphasise my appeal that you will kindly spare no effort to ensure the success of the undertaking.

I am,
Yours faithfully,
JOHN ADCOCK,
Hon. Sec.
