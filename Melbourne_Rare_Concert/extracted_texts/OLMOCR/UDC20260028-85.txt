TERCENTENARY of the PASSION PLAY
Oberammergau and the British Empire

AN INVITATION
(Extracts from a letter to the Press)

SIR:—

The presentation of the Passion Play at Oberammergau in 1930 made so deep an impression on the Executive Secretary of the National Council of Education of Canada that he was moved by a great desire that when next it was presented the greatest possible number of teachers and students from Canada might share his experience. This desire has gradually taken a wider range. Now, through the National Council of Education of Canada (and the Foreign Students Bureau at Berlin) the Municipality of Oberammergau has invited two thousand Educationists, Teachers and Students (including senior boys and girls of the Secondary schools and Teachers in Training) of the British Empire to attend a special performance of the Passion Play on August 10th, and, moreover, to be the guests of the villagers in their homes. The original invitation was for 5,000, the capacity of the new Auditorium, but at the suggestion of the National Council of Education it is now limited to the number mentioned.

The National Council of Education of Canada which, in conjunction with a special London Committee is undertaking the arrangements for this pilgrimage, proposes to organise a short Conference on "The Teaching of Christ and International Goodwill." The Conference will be preceded by a simple service in the great Auditorium, in which the Passion Play Orchestra and Choir will take part. The hope is that the urgent need of peace and goodwill throughout the world may be realised afresh in the wonderful atmosphere of Oberammergau and in the light of the great memories of Calvary and Easter.

Some words written by the Archbishop of Canterbury in a letter to the Executive Secretary may here be quoted:—"I have been greatly moved by the generous and gracious invitation of the people of Oberammergau which you are now authorised to extend to the teachers and students of the Empire. I cannot doubt that those to whom the invitation is addressed will be eager, so far as they can, to accept it. I hope that a great gathering may assemble at Oberammergau to receive for themselves the deep impression which the Passion Play cannot fail to give and with its inspiration to hasten the time when, forgetting the misunderstandings of the past, the nations may live together in mutual understanding and goodwill."

On behalf of the London Committee:—

Prayers

Let us pray for the Peace of the World.

Almighty God, from whom all thoughts of truth and peace proceed; kindle, we pray Thee, in the hearts of all men the true love of peace; and guide with Thy pure and peaceable wisdom those that take counsel for the nations of the earth; that in tranquillity Thy kingdom may go forward, till the earth is filled with the knowledge of Thy love; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Let us pray for Industrial Prosperity in all countries.

O God our Father, who through Thy Son Jesus Christ, has consecrated labour to the welfare of mankind, prosper, we pray Thee, the industries of all lands; bless all those who are engaged therein; shield them in all dangers and temptations, and grant that, receiving the due fruits of their labours, they may praise Thee by living according to Thy will; through the same Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Let us pray for the Universities and Schools.

Bless, O Lord, the work of all Universities, Colleges and Schools, that they who serve Thee therein, as teachers or learners, may set Thy holy will alway before them, and be led to the knowledge of Thy truth; that both Church and Commonwealth may be bettered by their studies and they themselves become true servants of Thy kingdom; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Let us pray for the spread of the Gospel of Christ.

O God, who hast made of one blood all nations of men for to dwell on the face of the earth, and didst send Thy blessed Son Jesus Christ to preach peace to them that are afar off and to them that are nigh; grant that all the peoples of the world may feel after Thee and find Thee; and hasten, O Lord, the fulfilment of Thy promise to pour out Thy spirit upon all flesh; through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Our Father, which art in Heaven, hallowed be Thy name, Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread, and forgive us our trespasses as we forgive them that trespass against us. And lead us not into temptation but deliver us from evil. For thine is the kingdom, the power and the glory, for ever and ever. Amen.

The Grace of our Lord Jesus Christ; the love of God and the fellowship of the Holy Ghost be with us evermore. Amen.

By Southern Railway special steamer (declassed) to Ostend; or L.N.E.R. to Hook of Holland or Flushing, thence by special trains to Munich and Oberammergau. The estimated cost for transportation only by one of these routes varies from £4 15s. to £6 10s. (Second Class).
A Committee consisting of the representatives of the different Transportation Companies and Travel Agencies concerned is at present engaged in the preparation of a memorandum setting forth the routes and special facilities available, together with definite costs. This, when printed, will be sent to all applicants.

=== Page 16 ===
At the time of going to Press, certain information is necessary before definite estimates of travel costs can be given. These latter will be sent to all applicants at the earliest possible moment. In the meantime, everything is being done to reduce the cost of transportation to and from Oberammergau to the utmost, and to this end the official co-operation of Government and transportation authorities is being secured.

The invitation, itself, it should be noted includes:—

(a) A seat at a special performance of the Passion Play as the guests of the Municipality of Oberammergau.

(b) Hospitality in the homes of the villagers from the afternoon of August 9th to the evening of August 10th.

(c) Participation in the special (broadcast) Service in the Passion Play Auditorium at 8.30 p.m. on August 9th in which the Passion Play Choir and Orchestra will participate.

(d) Attendance at the Conference in Munich on "The Teaching of Christ and International Goodwill."

(e) Participation in the official programme to be arranged for Bremen, Berlin, Munich and Paris.

As previously stated, it is expected that many of those applying for invitations will make their own plans for the journey to and from Oberammergau. To all accepted members a formal invitation will be sent and this card (printed in English and German) will serve for identity purposes throughout. For those travelling independently it must be produced when applying for the special facilities (including a reduction of 60%, for group or individual purposes) accorded by the German Railways which are available only through the offices of their official agent, Mr. C. J. Duncker, 66/68, Haymarket, London, S.W.1, or through the offices of Messrs. Thos. Cook & Son, and Messrs. Dean & Dawson, Ltd., the Directors of which have kindly placed the services of their international organizations at the disposal of the National Council of Education of Canada.

The formal itineraries, Nos. 1, 2 and 3, are drawn for School and other groups desiring to travel under exceptional conditions and to follow an organized programme.

The cost in each case, as at present estimated, is from £12 to £15. A definite amount will be fixed as soon as the necessary arrangements are completed.

In all cases a Registration Fee of 5s. will be charged to cover organisation and other expenses. Should any balance remain unspent it will be devoted to the Council's annual Summer School for Students in England to which it is proposed to invite a certain number of German, French and Italian girls during the Summer of 1935.
