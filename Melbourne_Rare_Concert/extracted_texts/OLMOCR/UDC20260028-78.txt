62nd Programme. Sixth Year, 1897-8
The Musical Society of Victoria.
[Instituted 1861. Re-Constituted 1892.]
Patrons:
His Excellency Lord Brassey, K.C.B.; Hon. Sir Frederick T. Sargood, K.C.M.G. M.L.C.; the Hon. Sir Arthur Snowden, K.B., M.L.C.; His Honor Mr. Justice E. D. Holroyd; His Honor Mr. Justice H. Hodges.
President:—Mr. E. A. Jäger.
The Ordinary Monthly Meeting will be held at Mr. Furlong's Rooms, Royal Arcade, on Saturday, February 26th, 1898, at 8 p.m.
PROGRAMME.
Under the direction of Mr. S. W. M. Lamble.
Duet (in canon) ... "Go Pretty Rose" ... ... Marzials
Mrs. Johnson and Miss Harvie.
Trio (piano violin and horn) Op. 40 ... ... Brahms
Andante—Scherzo—Adagio Mesto—Finale.
Messrs G. Peake, H. Curtis, H. Kuhr.
Songs ... ... (a) "Serenade" ... W. E. Nott
(b) "A Fragment" ... Miss Harvie.
Piano Solo ... ... Scherzo, Op 39, No.3 ... Chopin
Miss Mary Anderson.
Song ... "The Passing of the Soul" ... Lawson Marks
Mrs. Johnson.
Quartett (strings) ... Op. 16 ... Mozart
Allegro—Andante—Menuetto, and Trio
Allegro non troppo.
Messrs H. Curtis, V. Zelman, E. A. Jager, and Arthur Montague.
Accompanist: MR. W. E. NOTT.
It is requested that no one will enter or leave the Concert Room during the performance of any number.
W. H. HEATHCOTE, Hon. Secretary.
