06 HEARING THE ARCHIVE  ·  SIGNAL ANALYSIS

Structure

Every moment, compared to every other.

What it shows

A self-similarity matrix compares each moment of the recording with every other moment.

How to read it

Stripes parallel to the diagonal are passages that return later at the same speed; blocks are internally homogeneous sections.

Listen & look for

Repetition and form — verses, refrains, or a phrase that comes back minutes later.
