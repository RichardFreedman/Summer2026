05 HEARING THE ARCHIVE  ·  SIGNAL ANALYSIS

Tempogram

A spectrogram, but for rhythm.

What it shows

At every moment, shows how strongly the onsets repeat at every possible tempo.

How to read it

Bright horizontal bands are steady pulses; a fainter band at double or half tempo is the same pulse, counted differently.

Listen & look for

Whether the pulse holds steady through the excerpt, speeds up, or drifts.
