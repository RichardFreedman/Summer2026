02 HEARING THE ARCHIVE  ·  SIGNAL ANALYSIS

Spectrogram

Every frequency, frame by frame.

What it shows

Cuts the sound into short frames and shows how much energy sits at each frequency in each one.

How to read it

Bright horizontal bands are sustained tones and their harmonics; bright vertical lines are attacks.

Listen & look for

Timbral brightness, harmonic stacking, tonal vs. noisy texture.
