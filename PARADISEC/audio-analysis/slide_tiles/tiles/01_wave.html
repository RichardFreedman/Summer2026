01 HEARING THE ARCHIVE  ·  SIGNAL ANALYSIS

Waveform

Loudness and phrasing, moment by moment.

What it shows

Air pressure over time. At this zoom it shows loudness and phrasing — not pitch.

How to read it

The filled shape is the amplitude envelope; the orange line is smoothed loudness (RMS) in decibels below the loudest moment.

Listen & look for

Phrase boundaries, dynamic swells, breaths and silences.
