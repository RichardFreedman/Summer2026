07 HEARING THE ARCHIVE  ·  SIGNAL ANALYSIS

Layers

Sustained tone, separated from attack.

What it shows

Harmonic/percussive separation (HPSS) splits the spectrogram into held tones and transient attacks.

How to read it

The harmonic layer is smooth along time; the percussive layer is smooth along frequency. A share reports the split.

Listen & look for

Melody apart from accompaniment, or a voice apart from drums — play each layer on its own.
