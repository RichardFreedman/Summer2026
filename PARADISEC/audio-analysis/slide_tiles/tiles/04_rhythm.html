04 HEARING THE ARCHIVE  ·  SIGNAL ANALYSIS

Rhythm

Where the pulse begins, and where it lands.

What it shows

Onsets mark moments where something new begins; beats are the pulse tracked from them.

How to read it

Grey curve is onset strength; orange ticks are onsets; green ticks are the tracked beats.

Listen & look for

Whether a click track on the beats matches the pulse you feel — steady meter vs. free rhythm.
